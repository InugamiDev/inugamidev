## About

I am a full-stack developer and software engineer in Ho Chi Minh City. Frontend, backend, mobile,
IoT, Web3, research: I go wherever the problem is and build whatever it takes to solve it. The tools
change with the job; the approach stays the same: ship fast, build stable, iterate with users.

Right now I am CTO at [Admission Sorted](https://admissionsorted.com/), where a student builds one
profile, it is matched to each programme's entry requirements, and one application goes to many
universities, with portals for agents and universities. I am also the founder of 100B Studio, a
full-stack engineering studio.

Before that I built the five products behind [TwoHearts](https://twohearts.vn/), which lets
restaurants, cafes, homestays, tours and small brands in Vietnam sell inside ChatGPT for 5 to 10
percent instead of the quarter to a third delivery platforms take. I was Country Lead for
[Agora](https://agora.io) in Vietnam, and a full-stack engineer at [The Vorld](https://thevorld.com),
building Solana web3 infrastructure and the game integrations that put it on Twitch and YouTube.

A friend asked me to take on [Lotus Hack](https://www.lotushack.org/), Vietnam's largest hackathon
of 2026: 36 hours, 800+ participants from 30+ countries. I designed and shipped the onboarding and
live event platform end to end.

I am studying for a BSc in Information Technology at International University, VNU HCM, on the
twinning programme with the University of the West of England.
